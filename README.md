## 🌌 Overview

Standard cosmological models treat cosmic expansion either via an *ad hoc* static vacuum energy density ($\Lambda\text{CDM}$) or unified bulk-viscous dark fluids. Conventional causal Israel–Stewart viscous fluids suffer from severe empirical pathologies:
1. **The Cosmic Age Shortfall:** Intermediate-redshift stiff-fluid behavior ($w_{\mathrm{eff}} \to 1$) suppresses the integrated age of the universe to $t_0 \approx 11\text{--}12\text{ Gyr}$ (violating stellar globular cluster ages $t_0 \ge 13.5\text{ Gyr}$).
2. **Structure Damping:** Negative effective sound speeds wipe out sub-horizon linear matter perturbation growth ($f\sigma_8$).

This repository provides numerical proof and tools demonstrating that **scale-space metric enthalpy degradation**—where dissipation is localized strictly to cosmological horizon scales—resolves both crises:
* **Asymptotic de Sitter Attractor:** The dynamical phase space features a stable fixed-point attractor ($w_{\mathrm{eff}} \to -1$).
* **Consistent Cosmic Age:** Preserves standard early matter domination, yielding an integrated cosmic age of $t_0 = 13.784\text{ Gyr}$ ($z_t \approx 0.626$).
* **Sub-Horizon Preservation:** Leaves sub-horizon matter fluctuations to collapse under standard Newtonian Jeans gravity ($f(z) \approx \Omega_{\mathrm{m}}^\gamma, \gamma \approx 0.554$), conforming with observational $f\sigma_8$ surveys.
